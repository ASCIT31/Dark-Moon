responsabilité limitée trading as **ASC-IT**, registered at the RCS of Toulouse under number 840 356 570, registered

It allows the employees and contractors that Corporation designates (« **Designated Contributors** ») to contribute
to Darkmoon on its behalf. Each Designated Contributor also signs the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

## 1. Grant

Sections 1 to 9 of the [Individual Contributor License Agreement v1.0](CLA.md) apply to every Contribution a
Designated Contributor submits on behalf of Corporation, with Corporation as the licensor of the copyright and patent
rights it holds in them, as if « You » meant Corporation.

## 2. Designated Contributors

Corporation designates its contributors by their GitHub accounts in Schedule A and keeps that list up to date by
written notice to ASC. A removal takes effect for Contributions submitted after ASC receives the notice; it does not
revoke the licenses already granted.

## 3. Authority

The person signing for Corporation represents that they are authorised to bind Corporation, and that Corporation has
the rights needed to grant the licenses above for the Contributions of its Designated Contributors.
